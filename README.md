# 🧑‍💼 HR Analytics Dashboard

This project presents a **comprehensive HR Analytics Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, departmental trends, job roles, and key business risks associated with employee turnover.

The dashboard transforms raw HR data into meaningful insights using **Power Query, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, interactive slicers, dynamic KPIs, and Excel-based analytical logic**.

The project also includes the underlying dataset and a dashboard presentation that demonstrates the key findings, business problems, and recommended strategies for improving employee retention.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 👨‍💼 Employee Attrition by Age

Employees between the ages of **25–34** recorded the highest number of attrition cases, with approximately **112 exits**.

This indicates that early- and mid-career employees represent an important segment for retention analysis.

### 🏢 Attrition by Department

The dashboard highlights significant employee turnover across major departments:

- **R&D:** 133 exits
- **Sales:** 92 exits

R&D and Sales account for a substantial portion of the organization's recorded attrition.

### 💼 Attrition by Job Role

The job roles most affected by employee attrition include:

- Laboratory Technician
- Research Scientist
- Sales Executive

These roles can be examined further to identify potential patterns related to compensation, workload, career progression, job satisfaction, and working conditions.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Attrition by Gender

The dataset shows:

- **Male employees:** 63% of attrition
- **Female employees:** 37% of attrition

This demographic difference can be used as a starting point for further workforce analysis while considering the underlying employee population sizes.

## 🚨 Business Problems Identified

Based on the dashboard analysis, several workforce challenges were identified.

### 1. High Attrition Among Younger Employees

Employees aged **25–34** represent the age group with the highest number of exits.

Potential areas for investigation include:

- Career progression
- Compensation
- Training opportunities
- Job satisfaction
- Workload
- Work-life balance

### 2. High Employee Turnover in R&D

The **R&D department records the highest number of attrition cases**.

This makes it important to investigate factors such as:

- Career development opportunities
- Research workload
- Compensation
- Management
- Job satisfaction
- Opportunities for advancement

### 3. Significant Attrition in Sales

The **Sales department** also experiences substantial employee turnover.

Sales roles can involve demanding targets, performance pressure, and variable compensation, making employee engagement and incentive structures important areas for analysis.

### 4. Attrition Concentrated in Specific Job Roles

Laboratory Technicians, Research Scientists, and Sales Executives appear prominently among employees who left the organization.

A role-specific retention strategy can help HR teams better understand the reasons behind turnover.

# 🛠️ Proposed Solutions

## 🚀 1. Career Growth & Development

Organizations can improve employee retention by providing employees with clear opportunities for professional development.

### Recommended initiatives:

- Define clear promotion pathways
- Create structured career progression plans
- Introduce Learning & Development programs
- Provide technical and professional training
- Offer skill-based certifications
- Develop mentorship programs
- Conduct regular career development discussions

Clear career paths can help employees understand how they can progress within the organization.

---

## 🎖️ 2. Incentives & Employee Recognition

Employee recognition and appropriate incentives can support engagement and motivation.

### Recommended initiatives:

- Introduce performance-based bonuses
- Create employee recognition programs
- Implement **Employee of the Month** awards
- Recognize outstanding performance publicly
- Provide gift vouchers or additional paid leave
- Celebrate team and individual achievements
- Establish transparent performance recognition criteria

Recognition programs can help employees feel that their contributions are valued.

---

## 🧘 3. Work-Life Balance

Work-life balance can be an important consideration when evaluating employee retention.

### Recommended initiatives:

- Introduce flexible working hours where practical
- Provide remote or hybrid work options where suitable
- Monitor employee workloads
- Conduct employee wellness programs
- Provide health and wellness initiatives
- Encourage appropriate time off
- Establish programs that help employees manage workplace stress

The objective is to create a sustainable working environment while maintaining business productivity.

---

## 🗣️ 4. Employee Engagement & Feedback

Regular communication with employees can help organizations identify workplace concerns before they contribute to turnover.

### Recommended initiatives:

- Conduct regular employee satisfaction surveys
- Use anonymous feedback channels
- Conduct structured exit interviews
- Analyze common reasons for employee departures
- Hold regular one-on-one meetings
- Encourage open communication
- Track recurring employee concerns
- Develop action plans based on feedback

Employee feedback should be converted into measurable actions rather than simply collected.

# 📈 Recommended HR Analytics Approach

The dashboard can be used as a starting point for a broader HR analytics process.

### Step 1 — Identify High-Risk Segments

Use the dashboard to identify groups with higher levels of attrition based on:

- Age
- Department
- Job role
- Gender
- Income
- Job satisfaction
- Years at company
- Overtime
- Work-life balance

### Step 2 — Investigate Potential Drivers

After identifying high-attrition groups, investigate the underlying factors associated with employee turnover.

### Step 3 — Develop Targeted Retention Strategies

Instead of applying the same solution across the entire workforce, HR teams can develop targeted interventions for specific departments, roles, or employee groups.

### Step 4 — Monitor Results

Track attrition KPIs over time to determine whether retention initiatives are associated with changes in workforce turnover.
